Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our parcel tracking system.

How does the system track parcels?

Once the parcel is registered into the system, it is assigned a unique tracking number. This number can be used by both staff and customers to monitor the parcel's status at any time. The system sends status updates automatically as updated by the staff during the process.

How do customers track their parcels?

Customers can track parcels in multiple ways: through a tracking link sent via SMS, by entering their tracking number on our TunaTrack tracking page, or by calling your office where staff can quickly look up the status.

What is the pilot phase?

The pilot phase is a one month trial period where we test the system with willing businesses for free. This helps us gather feedback and make improvements before a full-scale launch.

How can my business participate in the pilot phase?

To participate in the pilot phase, your business can register interest through the contact form or email us and we will be in touch.

What are my costs during the pilot phase?

During the pilot phase, there are no costs for participating businesses. This trial period is completely free, allowing you to test the system and provide feedback without any financial commitment.
